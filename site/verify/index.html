Verify a certificate

Got a laptop or a drive with a ZeroTrace certificate? Drop the file here to check it's genuine and see what was done to the drive. It all happens in your browser. The file never leaves your computer.

Drop the certificate here, or choose a file

If whoever erased the drive gave you their public key (a .pub file), paste it here to confirm the certificate came from them.

What the result means

A valid signature means the certificate hasn't been changed since it was issued. It only tells you who issued it if the signing key matches a public key you already trust. Certificates marked self-attested were signed with a one-off key: the contents are intact, but anyone could have made one.

The check uses Ed25519 through your browser's built-in Web Crypto, over the RFC 8785 canonical form of the file. The format is described here.
